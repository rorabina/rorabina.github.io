Insights

Vision & Philosophy (The "Republic" Metaphor)
Viewing personal evolution, technical endeavors, and creative archives through the lens of a self-sustained state.
  • Just as a republic balances infrastructure, culture, and progress, this space unifies diverse disciplines—from frontier tech and social initiatives to visual storytelling—into an interconnected ecosystem built on autonomy, continuous research, and purpose-driven work.
  • Rooted in open-source principles, user-first design, sustainability, and continuous learning, the Republic is designed to mature far beyond a simple personal archive. The overarching goal is to cultivate a self-sustaining platform that collaborates with and supports like-minded individuals and communities both locally and globally. Through initiative-driven commerce—such as partnering with local artisans, customizing ethically sourced items, and reinvesting proceeds—this space directly funds forward-thinking projects, social causes, and grassroots livelihoods.
  • The "Republic" represents a deliberate framework: technical builds serve as the infrastructure, media acts as the record, and human-centric insights guide the direction. It is a long-term commitment to building accessible tools, documenting raw perspectives, and empowering others through shared value and creative independence.
The Sovereign behind the Republic
A quick snapshot of the creator's background, technical focus, and creative pursuits.
  • A multi-disciplinary builder, visual artist, and digital creator operating from Metro Manila, Philippines.
  • RJrb constructs and deploys technical solutions using tools like GitHub and Mobirise, backed by hands-on experience in specialized AI and data annotation platforms including SuperAnnotate, V7 Darwin, and Icecow. While technical workflows provide the structural foundation, visual arts and photography remain the core passion—born as quiet pastimes and refined through moments of solitude and self-reliance.
  • Influenced by archival literature, vintage editorial design, and classic editions of publications like Reader's Digest and Vogue, RJrb absorbs the timeless integration of lifestyle, architecture, technology, and fine arts. This unique convergence shapes a distinct visual language and strategic vision—blending technical precision, aesthetic depth, and social awareness into every project under the Republic.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview
Republic of Rabina is a personal portfolio website and digital media archive designed to showcase projects, articles, and creative work. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy outlines how content is served and how user data is handled.

2. Data Collection & Analytics
We do not collect, store, or process sensitive personal information (such as names, addresses, or payment details).

  • Hosting & Server Logs: This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard system logs from site visitors, including IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps, for technical operation, security auditing, and bandwidth performance. These logs are managed under GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Website Analytics: To better understand general readership and content performance, we may utilize privacy-friendly or anonymous analytics tools (such as aggregated pageview counts). These tools do not track individual identity, compile personal profiles, or sell data to third parties.

3. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Local Storage
When you install or use the Rorabina App on desktop or mobile devices, the application downloads and caches website assets (such as HTML, styles, images, and text content) directly onto your local device storage.

  • Purpose: This local caching is strictly used to enable full offline browsing capability so you can read articles and view portfolio content without an active internet connection.
  • Data Access: The app does not access, read, or upload any files, contacts, location data, or sensitive information stored on your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Links
The site may contain links to external third-party platforms (such as social media pages or external repositories). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.
