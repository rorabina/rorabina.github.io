Install App

Download the Rorabina App

You can install a lightweight version of this website directly onto your mobile device without using an app store.

This provides faster loading times and places an app icon on your home screen. It runs in a full-screen window without browser address bars, giving you a native app experience.

Android iOS

For iOS Users (via Apple Safari):

1. Scan the QR code using your Camera app or open Safari on your iPhone or iPad and go to rorabina.github.io.

2. Tap the Share button (the square icon with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of your screen).

3. Scroll down the menu options and tap Add to Home Screen.

4. Confirm the app title (Rorabina App) and tap Add in the top-right corner.

5. The logo icon will immediately appear on your iPhone home screen and open in full-screen mode without browser bars.
